About

I’m Jacob Schneider, a 19-year-old designer working across websites and brand identity. I started at 16 and have since worked with some big names. Everything I make is crafted by hand. I use AI tools where they help, but never let them do the thinking for me.

Timeline

  1. First websites Spent the Christmas holiday teaching myself WordPress and building my first sites. Mostly to find out how far I could push a layout before it broke.
  2. First paid gig Picked up my first client through Fiverr. Small job, but the first time someone paid for something I'd designed.
  3. ITGWO First serious project: the site for Into The Great Wide Open, the festival on Vlieland. It had to read as well on a phone in a field as on a desk.
  4. An offer from Framer Framer offered me a job. I had to turn it down — school came first. Still the clearest sign so far that the work was landing.
  5. Museum Villa Built Museum Villa together with Studio Somehow — my first proper studio collaboration, and the first time my work sat inside someone else's art direction.
  6. Mollie Spent the summer at Mollie, introduced by the founder himself. A year earlier I was building sites on my bedroom floor.
  7. Daniel Dalen Ongoing freelance work for Daniel Dalen through the rest of 2025 and into 2026 — the kind of repeat client that tells you the work holds up.
  8. Red Antler Worked with Red Antler, the New York studio, on a new project called MODA.
  9. Open for work Taking on freelance projects and part-time roles. If you're looking to level up your digital presence, let's talk.
